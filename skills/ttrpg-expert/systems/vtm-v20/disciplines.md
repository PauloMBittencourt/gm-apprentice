# Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary -- Disciplines

The supernatural powers of the Kindred, from V20 Chapter Four. Each
Discipline lists its five basic powers with cost, roll and a one-line
effect, then the names of its elder powers (6+). Thaumaturgy and
Necromancy list their paths and rituals by name.

This is a table aid, not the book. Effects are summarised in our own
words; check the page for the full write-up, tables and edge cases.

Clan Disciplines: `systems/vtm-v20/clans.md`. Experience costs:
`systems/vtm-v20/character-generation.md`. Dice, Willpower and blood:
`systems/vtm-v20/mechanics.md`.

Abbreviations: **BP** blood point, **WP** Willpower point, **diff**
difficulty, **succ** successes. "vs WP" means difficulty equal to the
target's *current* Willpower points.

## How Disciplines Work

- **Dots.** Disciplines are rated in dots. Each dot unlocks that
  level's power and keeps all lower ones. Ratings above 5 are possible
  only for vampires of the 7th Generation or lower (stronger blood).
- **Cost.** A power is free to use unless its entry says it costs
  blood or Willpower.
- **Starting dots.** A new character gets three dots among the three
  clan Disciplines. Caitiff may spread them over any Disciplines, as
  the Storyteller allows.
- **Learning out of clan.** The student needs a vampire teacher, who
  must feed the student one BP of their blood before the first lesson.
  Raising a Discipline the character already has needs no blood.
- **Elder powers (6+).** At levels 6 and up a Discipline can offer
  several powers. The vampire picks one; each further power at that
  level costs the same experience as buying the dot again. The
  Storyteller may allow home-made elder powers. These powers can
  unbalance a chronicle, so allow them with care.
- **Celerity, Fortitude and Potence 6+.** An elder either keeps
  raising the basic bonus or takes an alternate power instead, giving
  up that dot's bonus. They can buy the missed dot later.

*Source: V20 p. 127; elder powers pp. 127, 142, 158, 191*

### Eye Contact

Many mental powers (most of Dominate, some Animalism, Dementation and
Presence) need eye contact. What matters is catching the target's
attention, not the literal eyes. A target trying to avoid it rolls
Willpower against a difficulty of the user's Manipulation +
Intimidation (or another fitting pair). Covering the eyes lowers that
difficulty: −1 for sunglasses or closed eyes, up to −3 for a thick
blindfold or no eyes at all. The Storyteller has the final word.

*Source: V20 p. 152*

### Resisting Dominate

- **Generation.** A vampire can't Dominate a Kindred of lower
  Generation (stronger blood). The user's Generation must be equal or
  lower.
- **Mortals.** Very few resist. True Faith, psychic gifts or rare
  willpower can (True Faith: p. 372).
- **Nature.** Commands against the target's Nature work badly or not
  at all. Empathic Natures are easier to sway, willful ones harder.
- **Botch.** The target is immune to that vampire's Dominate for the
  rest of the story.

*Source: V20 pp. 151, 156-157*

## Celerity

Each dot adds one die to every Dexterity roll. By spending 1 BP at the
start of a turn (even beyond the Generation per-turn limit), the
vampire can turn dots into extra full physical actions, one per dot,
taken at the end of the turn. Dots used for actions don't add
Dexterity dice that turn. Extra actions can't be mental (no double
Dominate) and can't be split again.

**Elder alternates:** 6 Projectile; 7 Flower of Death; 8 Zephyr.

*Source: V20 pp. 141-143*

## Fortitude

Each dot adds to Stamina for soaking bashing and lethal damage. It
also lets the vampire soak aggravated damage (fire, sunlight, claws,
fangs) with Fortitude dice alone, which plain Stamina can't do. Soak
rules: `rules-reference.md` and V20 p. 272.

**Elder alternates:** 6 Personal Armor; 7 Shared Strength;
8 Adamantine.

*Source: V20 pp. 157-158*

## Necromancy

rituals.

  dots in the primary; a third path needs the primary mastered at 5.
- **Rolls.** Each path power has its own cost and roll; see the

- **Rituals.** Intelligence + Occult, diff 3 + level (max 9). A new

## Obfuscate

Not invisibility: the observer's mind ignores the vampire. Cameras
record what is really there, but the person filming is still fooled
and only sees the vampire on playback later, if at all. To spot an
Obfuscated vampire, an observer needs Auspex higher than the user's
Obfuscate and a Perception + Awareness roll, diff 7 minus the
difference between the two ratings.

| 3 | Mask of a Thousand Faces | -- (extra BP to look more attractive) | Manipulation + Performance, diff 7 | Seen as someone else. Impersonating a real person needs a good look at them first |

## Potence

Each dot adds one die to every Strength roll. By spending 1 BP, the
vampire turns those dice into the same number of automatic successes
for the turn. In melee and brawling, Potence successes (rolled or
automatic) add to the damage result.

**Elder alternates:** 6 Imprint; 7 Earthshock; 8 Flick.

*Source: V20 pp. 191-192*

## Thaumaturgy

Tremere blood magic, split into **paths** (effects at will) and
**rituals** (formal spells).

**Paths:**

- **Primary path.** On learning Thaumaturgy the character picks a
  primary path, gets its first dot and one level 1 ritual. Each new
  Thaumaturgy dot raises the primary path too.
- **Secondary paths.** They open once the primary path has two dots,
  and are bought separately (cost: `character-generation.md`). The
  primary must stay at least one dot above every secondary until it
  reaches 5.
- **Limits.** A path never goes above 5, even when Thaumaturgy does.
- **Using a path power.** Spend 1 BP and roll Willpower, diff = power
  level + 3. One success makes it work; some powers scale with
  successes. A botch backfires badly.

**Rituals:**

- **Learning.** Rituals are learned in play from a teacher or a text.
  There is no experience cost, but only up to the character's
  Thaumaturgy rating.
- **Casting.** Intelligence + Occult, diff 3 + ritual level (max 9).
  Casting takes 5 minutes per level unless the ritual says otherwise.
  Many rituals need components.
- **Failure.** A failed ritual can seem to work and fail later. A
  botch can be a disaster.

*Source: V20 pp. 211-212, 229*

Path of Blood in play:

- **A Taste for Blood:** touch blood to learn how much is
  left, how recently a vampire fed, and their rough Generation. With 3+
  successes, also whether they have committed diablerie.
- **Blood Rage:** force a vampire to spend blood as the
  caster chooses (past their per-turn limit). Each BP raises their
  frenzy difficulty by 1.
- **Blood of Potency:** lower the caster's own Generation for an hour
  or more.
- **Theft of Vitae:** pull blood from a visible target within 50 ft,
  as if drinking it. It can't blood bond the caster, and it is a blatant
  Masquerade breach.
- **Cauldron of Blood:** by touch, boil the target's blood: one
  aggravated level per BP boiled (Fortitude alone soaks). One success
  kills a mortal.

Other path notes:

- **Lure of Flames:** the fire must be released to burn. Aggravated
  damage per turn and soak diff: candle 1 (3), palm of flame 1 (4),
  campfire 2 (5), bonfire 2 (7), inferno 3 (9). Successes set how
  accurately it lands.
- **Movement of the Mind:** each success is a turn of control. A
  living target resists with opposed Willpower each turn. Level 3 lets
  the caster fly at running speed; level 4 lets them throw with
  Strength equal to the path.
- **Weather Control:** affects an area a few miles across. Successes
  set how fast the change comes (1 = up to a day, 5 = at once). Fog
  and rain impair sight and hearing (+1 and +2 diff); a storm is rain
  plus high winds. Lightning does 10 dice of lethal damage, and aiming
  it needs Perception + Occult, diff 6 (open ground) to 10 (indoors by
  a window). Indoors, +2 diff.

**Thaumaturgical Countermagic** is a separate Discipline. It is
reflexive and needs no split pool. To counter a path power or ritual,
the user needs a Countermagic rating at least equal to its level, then
spends 1 BP and rolls Countermagic dice at the power's difficulty;
each success cancels one of the caster's. It works at half dice
against other magic, can be learned by those who can't learn
Thaumaturgy, and grants no rituals.

*Source: V20 pp. 212-228; Countermagic p. 228*

## Running Disciplines at the Table

- **Say what it looks like.** Many powers are invisible (Dominate,
  Obfuscate, Auspex) and some are blatant (Feral Claws, Theft of
  Vitae, Horrid Form). Blatant ones in public are Masquerade breaches
  with consequences.
- **Let investigators keep the trail.** In a mystery, a power should
  speed up or deepen a clue, not be the only way to find it.
- **Secret rolls.** Roll Aura Perception, The Spirit's Touch and
  Entrancement yourself when the player shouldn't know how well it
  worked.
- **Track blood.** Most strong powers cost blood, and the Generation
  limit caps how much a vampire can spend per turn
  (`mechanics.md`). An empty pool brings hunger frenzy.
- **Botches stick.** A botched Dominate or Entrancement makes that
  target immune to the user for the story or night. Note it.

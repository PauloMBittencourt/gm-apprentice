# Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary -- Abilities Reference

The 30 Abilities (10 Talents, 10 Skills, 10 Knowledges), what each
covers, and the common dramatic systems that use them. Use it at
character creation and whenever you need to pick the right
Attribute + Ability pair for a roll.

How dice pools, difficulty and specialties work is in
`mechanics.md`. Dot allocation (7/5/3, 3 max before freebies) is in
`character-generation.md`. Combat rolls are in `rules-reference.md`.

## Rolling Without the Ability

| Category | Learned by | No dots in it |
|---|---|---|
| **Talents** | Doing; experience, not study | Roll the Attribute alone, no penalty |
| **Skills** | Training or apprenticeship | Roll the Attribute at **+1 difficulty** |
| **Knowledges** | Study (school or self-taught) | **No roll** unless the Storyteller allows it (common trivia, say) |

**Must specialise:** Expression, Crafts, Performance, Academics, Law,
Science and Technology work best with a specialty picked even below 4
dots; Crafts and Performance always name one.

*Source: V20 pp. 100, 103, 107*

## Talents

| Talent | Covers | Usual pair | Example specialties |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alertness | Noticing physical things you weren't looking for | Perception | Noises, eavesdropping, hidden weapons, crowds |
| Athletics | Running, jumping, climbing, swimming, throwing, sports (not lifting; not combat) | Dexterity, Stamina | Swimming, climbing, acrobatics, parkour |
| Awareness | Sensing the supernatural, distinct from Alertness and Occult | Perception | Ghostly activity, mystical objects, mental intrusion |
| Brawl | Unarmed fighting, trained or street | Dexterity, Strength | Dirty fighting, throws, holds, a martial art |
| Empathy | Reading feelings and motives, spotting lies, seeming sympathetic | Perception, Charisma | Emotions, motives, gaining trust |
| Expression | Getting a point across: speech, writing, acting, posting | Manipulation, Charisma | Acting, poetry, fiction, social media |
| Intimidation | Threats, force of personality, pulling rank | Manipulation, Strength | Veiled threats, blackmail, physical coercion |
| Leadership | Inspiring others to follow | Charisma | Oratory, military, noble |
| Streetwise | Blending in on the street, gossip, slang, crime contacts | Charisma, Wits | Drugs, illegal weapons, gangs, local slang |
| Subterfuge | Hiding your motives, lying, intrigue, seduction | Manipulation | Seduction, lies, feigning mortality, the long con |

*Source: V20 pp. 100-103*

## Knowledges

| Knowledge | Covers | Usual pair | Example specialties |
|---|---|---|---|
| Academics | Humanities: history, literature, art, philosophy | Intelligence | A period, an art, a language family |
| Computer | Operating and programming computers and mobile devices; Internet sense | Intelligence, Wits | Internet, databases, programming, viruses |
| Finance | Commerce, value, money markets, laundering | Intelligence, Manipulation | Stock market, laundering, accounting, fencing |
| Investigation | Spotting details, research, following leads | Perception, Intelligence | Forensics, search, database research |
| Law | Statutes and procedure; also the Traditions | Intelligence, Charisma | Criminal, contracts, police procedure, the Traditions |
| Medicine | Human (and some vampire) anatomy, first aid, diagnosis, drugs | Intelligence, Dexterity | Emergency care, pathology, poisons, the Kindred condition |
| Occult | Mysticism, magic, folklore and especially vampire lore; rumour as much as fact | Intelligence | Kindred lore, rituals, infernalism, Noddist lore |

Knowledges are usually paired with Mental Attributes; other pairs
(Stamina + Occult to keep chanting through the day) are possible but
rare.

*Source: V20 pp. 107-110*

## Secondary Abilities

For anything the 30 don't cover, the Storyteller can allow a
**Hobby Talent** (self-taught, mostly active: carousing, scrounging,
fortune-telling), a **Professional Skill** (trained trade) or an
**Expert Knowledge** (studied field). Check first whether it's really
a specialty of an existing Ability -- swimming is Athletics,
forensics is Investigation.

*Source: V20 pp. 103, 107, 110*

## Sample Rolls

The book's own examples show how freely Attributes and Abilities mix:

| Situation | Roll | Diff |
|---|---|---|
| Behaving flawlessly at a formal dinner you can't eat | Dexterity + Etiquette | 8 |
| Finding shelter before dawn, far from your haven | Wits + Survival | 7 |
| Staring down a gang leader in front of his gang | Charisma + Intimidation, resisted | -- |
| Threatening a rival in Elysium without saying it | Manipulation + Intimidation | 8 |
| Holding still in the bushes (1 hour per success) | Stamina + Stealth | 7 |
| Asking the streets about a new gang | Charisma + Streetwise | 8 |
| Spotting the alarm system | Perception + Larceny | 6 |
| Which story will the Prince believe? | Manipulation + Expression, resisted | -- |
| Outrunning pursuers (extended, 15 successes) | Stamina + Athletics | 7 |
| Pleading with a judge before sunrise | Charisma + Law | 8 |

*Source: V20 pp. 254-255*

## Dramatic Systems

Ready-made rolls for common actions. Most can be retried after a
failure at +1 difficulty (see "Trying It Again" in `mechanics.md`).

### Physical

- **Hunting** (any pair that fits the method; one roll per hour): the
  pair follows how the player describes the hunt -- Appearance +
  Subterfuge for a nightclub seduction, Stamina + Athletics for a
  chase through a park, Wits + Streetwise for cat-and-mouse.
  Difficulty by area:

  | Area | Diff |
  |---|---|
  | Slum, the Rack | 4 |
  | Lower-income or bohemian district | 5 |
  | Downtown business district, warehouses | 6 |
  | Suburb | 7 |
  | Heavily patrolled area | 8 |

  Success: prey found and subdued, take as much as you want (or one
  die of blood points, if the ST prefers). Failure: an hour wasted.
  Botch: trouble -- a dead vessel, a disease, a rival's domain, a
  street gang. Play the botch out as a scene. Herd and Fame make
  hunting easier (`character-generation.md`).
- **Climbing** (Dexterity + Athletics): usually extended. Claws from
  Protean or Vicissitude make it easier.
- **Jumping** (Strength; Strength + Athletics with a run-up).
- **Pursuit** (Dexterity + Athletics or Drive): if one side is clearly
  faster, just compare movement; otherwise run it as an extended
  contest.
- **Shadowing** (Dexterity + Stealth, or Drive in a car) against the
  target's Perception + Alertness, diff 6 for both. The target must
  get **more** successes to spot the tail.
- **Sneaking** (Dexterity + Stealth) against observers' Perception +
  Alertness, diff 6. Unless observers get more successes, the
  character passes. Noise, gear and open ground raise the Stealth
  difficulty; cameras or good vantage points add dice to the
  watchers. Obfuscate may make the roll unnecessary.
- **Intrusion** (Dexterity or Perception + Larceny), diff 5 (ordinary
  lock) to 9 (a vault). Active security must be beaten on the **first
  try** or the alarm goes off; plain locks can be retried. Some jobs
  need a minimum Larceny, and most need tools. Hacking the alarm
  itself is Technology, not Larceny.
- **Swimming** (Stamina + Athletics), **Throwing** (Dexterity +
  Athletics).

*Source: V20 pp. 258-262*

### Mental

- **Investigation** (Perception + Investigation): one success gives
  the basics; more give detail and deductions. A botch misses or
  ruins the obvious clue.
- **Tracking** (Perception + Survival): following physical signs. The
  quarry can cover their trail with Wits + Survival; each success adds
  +1 to the tracker's difficulty. City streets and bad weather make it
  harder.
- **Computer Use** (Intelligence or Wits + Computer), **Repair**
  (Dexterity or Perception + Crafts).
- **Awakening** by day: see "Daytime" in `rules-reference.md`.

*Source: V20 pp. 262-265*

### Social

- **Credibility** (Manipulation + Subterfuge to run a scam, Perception
  + Subterfuge to see through one), usually diff 7. The marks must
  beat the con artist's successes. Good props make the scam harder to
  spot.
- **Fast-Talk** (Manipulation + Subterfuge) against the target's
  Willpower, diff 6. Ties mean more babbling.
- **Interrogation:**
  - Peaceful: Manipulation + Empathy against the subject's Willpower,
    diff 6, rolled at key moments.
  - Violent: Manipulation + Intimidation against the higher of the
    subject's Stamina + 3 or Willpower, every turn or minute. The
    subject loses 1 health level per turn of physical torture or 1
    Willpower per turn of mental torture. A botch can break body or
    mind for good.
- **Intimidation:**
  - Passive: no roll; people simply give you room.
  - Subtle: Manipulation + Intimidation against Willpower, diff 6.
  - Blatant: Strength + Intimidation against the higher of Willpower
    or the target's Strength + Intimidation, diff 6.
  - A botch makes you look ridiculous for the rest of the scene.
- **Oration** (Charisma + Leadership), **Performance** (Charisma +
  Performance), **Carousing** (Charisma + Empathy, getting people to
  relax -- handy for feeding).
- **Seduction**, three rolls in sequence. Each success after the first
  adds a die to the next roll:

  | Step | Roll | Difficulty |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1. Approach | Appearance + Subterfuge | Target's Wits + 3 |
  | 2. Repartee | Wits + Subterfuge | Target's Intelligence + 3 |
  | 3. Intimate talk | Charisma + Empathy | Target's Perception + 3 |

  A failure at step 1 is disinterest (a botch, disgust). A failure at
  step 2 leaves the door open for later. Success at step 3 means they
  go somewhere private; roleplay the rest.

*Source: V20 pp. 265-266*

## Choosing the Roll at the Table

- **Pick the Ability from what the character is doing**, then the
  Attribute from how they do it. Charisma charms, Manipulation steers,
  Appearance impresses; Wits reacts, Perception notices, Intelligence
  reasons.
- **Don't roll what isn't in doubt.** With 1+ dot and no pressure,
  most everyday uses just happen.
- **Social rolls don't replace roleplay.** Let the player say what
  their character does, then set the difficulty from how well it fits.
- **Hunting is a scene, not a chore.** Roll it quickly when it doesn't
  matter, and play it out when the method or a botch says something
  about the character.

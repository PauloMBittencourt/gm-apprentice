# Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary -- Humanity and Paths

Morality in V20: degeneration checks, the Humanity scale, the
alternate Virtues (Conviction and Instinct), and the Paths of
Enlightenment most often seen at the table. Use it whenever a
character does something that might cost Humanity or Path, when
building a Path follower, and when a botched Virtue roll calls for a
derangement.

Frenzy and Rötschreck rolls are in `rules-reference.md`. Virtue and
Humanity starting values and experience costs are in
`character-generation.md`.

**Coverage:** Humanity in full; eight Paths in full (Blood, Bones,
Caine, Cathari, Feral Heart, Honorable Accord, Night, Power and the
Inner Voice); the other core Paths by name and page only.

## Degeneration

When a character does something the Storyteller judges morally
questionable, look it up on the character's Hierarchy of Sins. If the
act sits **at or below the character's current rating**, a roll is
due. The higher the rating, the more acts count as sins; a vampire
far down the scale shrugs off small cruelties.

- **Roll:** Conscience (or Conviction), **difficulty 8**. The
  Storyteller may adjust it.
- **No Willpower** may be spent for an automatic success.
- **Any success:** no loss. The character feels remorse, or justifies
  the act to themselves.
- **Failure:** lose 1 dot of Humanity (or Path).
- **Botch:** lose 1 Humanity (or Path) **and** 1 Conscience (or
  Conviction), and gain a derangement of the Storyteller's choice.
- **Zero:** at Humanity 0 the Beast owns the character, and they
  become a Storyteller character.

"Violation" in the hierarchies is left vague on purpose: killing,
feeding by force, maiming or any other villainy can count.

Humanity and Path ratings go up **only with experience points**.

**Table practice the book asks for:**

- Warn the player before an act that would call for a roll, so the
  choice is theirs. Do the same for a frenzying character who is about
  to do something terrible (they can still spend Willpower to hold
  off the frenzy for a turn).
- Once you have warned them, roll. Don't threaten and then let it go.
- Roll consistently but not constantly. A stream of failed rolls
  turns tragedy into bookkeeping.
- At Humanity 4 or lower, the Storyteller may have the Beast push
  the character towards urges that need a Conscience roll or a point
  of Willpower to resist.

*Source: V20 pp. 309-311*

## Bearing

A vampire's morality shows as a vague impression others pick up.
Very high or very low ratings change the difficulty of the rolls tied
to that Path's bearing. For Humanity, that is Social rolls to seem
normal or win sympathy. The modifier can apply to Discipline rolls
where it fits.

| Rating | Bearing modifier |
|---|---|
| 10 | -2 difficulty |
| 9-8 | -1 difficulty |
| 7-4 | none |
| 3-2 | +1 difficulty |
| 1 | +2 difficulty |

*Source: V20 p. 310*

## Humanity

The default morality: not saintliness, just the ability to pass for
human and keep the Beast leashed. Most vampires, including most
Sabbat and independents, are on Humanity, only lower on it.

**What the rating does:**

- **Daytime:** a vampire forced to act by day can roll no more dice
  than their Humanity on any action. Higher Humanity also wakes earlier
  in the evening.
- **Virtue cap:** a Virtue roll can't use more dice than the
  character's Humanity.
- **Torpor:** the lower the Humanity, the longer the torpor (table in
  `rules-reference.md`).
- **Appearance:** low Humanity shows -- sunken eyes, a fixed snarl,
  and by 3-2 a pallid, corpselike look.
- **Zero:** no longer playable.

### Hierarchy of Sins -- Humanity

| Rating | The sin that triggers a roll |
|---|---|
| 10 | Selfish thoughts |
| 9 | Small selfish acts |
| 8 | Hurting someone, even by accident |
| 7 | Stealing |
| 6 | An accidental violation (draining a vessel dry while starving) |
| 5 | Deliberately destroying property |
| 4 | A violation in the heat of passion (manslaughter, a kill in frenzy) |
| 3 | A planned violation (murder, savouring a kill) |
| 2 | A casual violation (killing without thought, feeding past need) |
| 1 | Utter perversion and atrocity |

### The Humanity Scale

| Rating | Label | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Saintly | Rarer than among mortals; peers find them unbearable |
| 9 | Compassionate | Avoids killing, takes only the blood it needs |
| 8 | Caring | Exacting standards, clear sense of right and wrong |
| 7 | Normal | Where most mortals sit; can pass for human |
| 6 | Removed | Accepts that people die and things break when it needs blood |
| 5 | Distant | Unpleasant company; others' rights barely register |
| 4 | Unfeeling | Killing is fine if the victim "deserves" it; many elders sit here |
| 3 | Cold | Others' lives and property are irrelevant; twisted pleasures |
| 2 | Bestial | Perversion and mutilation; damnation is close |
| 1 | Horrific | Barely sentient; many can't speak coherently |
| 0 | Monstrous | Feed, sleep, kill. Lost to the Beast |

*Source: V20 pp. 311-313*

## Paths of Enlightenment

Codes of ethics for vampires who have given up on being human. They
work like Humanity mechanically, but each has its own Hierarchy of
Sins, its own bearing and sometimes different Virtues.

**Before allowing one:**

- **Paths are rare and secret.** Most vampires, even Sabbat, are on a
  low Humanity, not a Path. Followers recruit candidates; you don't
  apply.
- **They are hard to play.** The player has to think like something
  that is no longer human.
- **They are not a licence to murder.** Use them when the story or the
  character calls for one, not to dodge degeneration rolls.

*Source: V20 pp. 313-314*

### Conviction and Instinct

Some Paths swap the human Virtues for inhuman ones. A character never
has both Conscience and Conviction, nor both Self-Control and Instinct.

- **Conviction** (replaces Conscience): rolled for degeneration, like
  Conscience. Instead of remorse, the vampire acknowledges the failure
  and plans to do better. It marks the vampire as a monster: other
  supernatural beings that sense the Beast see it at once, and the
  vampire passes for human only on a casual look.
- **Instinct** (replaces Self-Control): controls the Beast by riding
  it, not denying it.
  - Facing frenzy, the character **always** frenzies, unless the
    difficulty to resist is lower than their Instinct; then they
    choose.
  - In frenzy the Beast drives, but to take a deliberate action
    ("riding the wave") the player rolls Instinct against the frenzy
    difficulty.
  - The frenzy otherwise works normally, wound penalties ignored and
    all.
- Anywhere a roll names Conscience or Self-Control, use Conviction or
  Instinct instead.

*Source: V20 pp. 314-315*

### Starting on a Path

For a new character who begins on a Path:

- **Inhuman Virtues start at 0.** Only the human Virtues kept (and
  Courage) get the free dot. The 7 Virtue dots must buy each inhuman
  Virtue to at least 1.
- **Willpower 5 minimum**, through Courage or freebie points.
- **Path rating 5 maximum** at creation, even if the Virtues add up
  higher, and freebies can't raise it past 5. Older characters may
  start higher if the Storyteller allows.

*Source: V20 p. 315*

### Leaving Humanity for a Path

1. **Requirements:** Humanity 3 or lower, and 1 dot in each Virtue
   that will change.
2. **Recruitment:** a follower of the Path approaches the character;
   play it out.
3. **Study:** at least a year under a mentor on the Path, while the
   character wears their Humanity and Virtues down to the required
   levels.
4. **Test:** ordeals, riddles or lore set by the Storyteller, always
   including an act that would be an atrocity in human terms, done
   to teach a lesson rather than for its own sake.
5. **Roll Willpower** (no spending Willpower for a success).
   Difficulty: Humanity 3 → 8, Humanity 2 → 7, Humanity 1 → 6.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Success | Human Virtues replaced by the Path's at 1; Path rating 1 |
| 3+ successes | As above, but Path rating 2 |
| Failure | A pointless atrocity: lose 1 Humanity; may retry in a month at the new difficulty |
| Botch | Lose 1 Humanity; no new attempt for a year |

If this process drops Humanity to 0, the character is lost to the
Beast.

*Source: V20 pp. 315-316*

## The Paths

Each entry gives the core idea, a few ethics in our own words, and
the Hierarchy of Sins condensed to one line per rating. The book adds
history, current practices, and the abilities and Disciplines
followers favour.

### Path of Blood (Dervishes)

**Virtues:** Conviction, Self-Control. **Bearing:** Resolve (affects
Willpower rolls).

Assamite assassins only, and only the loyal ones learn it. Other
Kindred are cursed descendants of Caine who must be converted to
Haqim's teaching or destroyed, and their blood collected.

**Ethics:** follow Haqim's teachings; feed on mortals only when you
must; take Kindred vitae whenever you can; master the Beast; seek lore
that helps the clan; offer other Kindred conversion and use those who
refuse.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Killing a mortal to feed |
| 9 | Breaking your word to a clanmate |
| 8 | Not offering a non-Assamite the chance to convert |
| 7 | Leaving an unrepentant Kindred outside the clan alive |
| 6 | Giving in to frenzy |
| 5 | Neglecting the lore of Caine (Khayyin) |
| 4 | Not demanding blood as payment |
| 3 | Refusing to help a more advanced follower |
| 2 | Not paying the blood tithe |
| 1 | Acting against another Assamite |

*Source: V20 pp. 316-318*

### Path of the Bones (Gravediggers)

**Virtues:** Conviction, Self-Control. **Bearing:** Silence (Stealth
and going unnoticed).

Giovanni necromancers who treat death as the one universal truth and
study it coldly. The Sabbat's **Path of Death and the Soul** (a
Tzimisce variant) uses the same rules.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Showing fear of death |
| 9 | Passing up a chance to study a death |
| 8 | Killing by accident |
| 7 | Putting off feeding when hungry |
| 6 | Giving in to frenzy |
| 5 | Declining a chance to kill |
| 4 | Deciding on emotion instead of logic |
| 3 | Going out of your way for someone else |
| 2 | Preventing a death without need |
| 1 | Actively stopping a death |

*Source: V20 pp. 318-320*

### Path of Caine (Noddists)

**Virtues:** Conviction, Instinct. **Bearing:** Faith (rolls that
rely on being seen as a devout scholar of Caine).

Scholars who study the Book of Nod and treat Caine as the model to
grow towards. Demanding and bookish: study every night, master the
Beast, shed what is left of the human.

**Ethics:** research Caine's history and learn from it; master both
will and Beast; take the blood of the unworthy to draw closer to
Caine; adapt to being a vampire; probe the limits of your powers.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | A night without research or study |
| 9 | Not teaching the Path to other vampires |
| 8 | Befriending or living alongside mortals |
| 7 | Disrespecting other students of Caine |
| 6 | Failing to ride the wave in frenzy |
| 5 | Giving in to Rötschreck |
| 4 | Passing up the chance to diablerize a "humane" vampire |
| 3 | Not testing the limits of your abilities |
| 2 | Neglecting lore about vampirism |
| 1 | Denying your vampiric needs |

*Source: V20 pp. 320-322*

### Path of Cathari (Albigensians)

**Virtues:** Conviction, Instinct. **Bearing:** Seduction (rolls to
tempt or seduce).

Rooted in the medieval Cathar heresy: the material world belongs to
evil, and vampires are its agents. Followers indulge every vice and
lead others into temptation.

**Ethics:** indulge in wealth, flesh and power; tempt others; Embrace
people of great passion; expect betrayal from everyone; accept your
role as a creature of evil; avoid Final Death.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Showing restraint |
| 9 | Trusting someone |
| 8 | Not passing the Curse to the passionately wicked or virtuous |
| 7 | Failing to ride the wave in frenzy |
| 6 | Acting against another Albigensian |
| 5 | Killing in passion |
| 4 | Giving up your pleasure for someone else |
| 3 | Holding back from indulgence |
| 2 | Killing for no reason |
| 1 | Encouraging others to show restraint |

*Source: V20 pp. 322-324*

### Path of the Feral Heart (Beasts)

**Virtues:** Conviction, Instinct. **Bearing:** Menace (rolls to
frighten or cow).

The vampire as the perfect predator: the Beast is just the hunter's
instinct, and denying it goes against nature. Followers balance
savagery with cunning and stay fiercely loyal to their pack. Its old
name was the **Path of Harmony** (sidebar, p. 325).

**Ethics:** survive first; politics and technology get in the way of
the hunt; strike a balance with the Beast; master the fear of fire;
give absolute loyalty to your pack.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Hunting with anything but your own vampiric gifts |
| 9 | Taking part in politics |
| 8 | Staying near fire or sunlight, unless it's to kill an enemy |
| 7 | Needless cruelty |
| 6 | Not hunting when hungry |
| 5 | Letting down your pack or allies |
| 4 | Killing without need |
| 3 | Ignoring your instincts |
| 2 | Killing for anything but survival |
| 1 | Refusing to kill to survive |

*Source: V20 pp. 324-326*

### Path of Honorable Accord (Knights)

**Virtues:** Conscience, Self-Control. **Bearing:** Devotion (rolls
to resist being manipulated or forced away from their purpose).

A hard warrior's code in place of human ethics: keep your word,
serve the cause, stand by your comrades. Knights rarely lead, because
they find politics distasteful, but they are at the front of every
fight.

**Ethics:** keep your word; never show cowardice; duty before self;
deal fairly with equals and betters; repay every debt; support your
comrades in everything except treachery.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|

| 9 | Not offering hospitality to allies |
| 8 | Keeping company with the dishonourable |
| 7 | Skipping your group's rituals |
| 6 | Disobeying your leader |
| 5 | Failing to protect your allies |
| 4 | Putting personal concerns before duty |
| 3 | Cowardice |
| 2 | Killing without reason |
| 1 | Breaking your word or oath |

*Source: V20 pp. 326-328*

### Path of Night (Nihilists)

**Virtues:** Conviction, Instinct. **Bearing:** Darkness (rolls to
bring out someone's inner darkness, such as dragging secrets from a
respected politician).

Built on Catholic damnation: vampires are damned by God to be agents
of evil, and their job is to spread dread and expose sin. Mostly
Lasombra.

**Ethics:** taint everyone you touch; tempt and horrify, so the weak
fall and the strong are tested; lead others to accept their darkness;
seek every sensation; death is a tool, not a goal.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Killing a mortal to feed |
| 9 | Acting in someone else's interest |
| 8 | Repeating yourself in your cruelties |
| 7 | Asking for help |
| 6 | Killing by accident |
| 5 | Bending to another Kindred's will |
| 4 | Killing on purpose or in passion |
| 3 | Helping someone |
| 2 | Accepting that another is your superior |
| 1 | Repenting |

*Source: V20 pp. 331-333*

### Path of Power and the Inner Voice (Unifiers)

**Virtues:** Conviction, Instinct. **Bearing:** Command (rolls to
lead or command).

Immortal, powerful and thirsty, the only real existence is as an
overlord. Followers chase control, reward rarely, punish failure
openly, and respect authority only while it holds.

**Ethics:** never tolerate failure, yours included; reward
unpredictably; seek control by any means; use the most effective tool
(fear and hate included) while appearing kind; respect those above
you but replace them when they slip; never show weakness.

| Rating | Sin |
|---|---|
| 10 | Denying responsibility for your actions |
| 9 | Treating subordinates badly |
| 8 | Disrespecting your superiors |
| 7 | Helping others when you gain nothing |
| 6 | Accepting defeat |
| 5 | Not killing when it serves you |
| 4 | Giving in to others' mistakes |
| 3 | Not using the most effective means |
| 2 | Leaving failure unpunished |
| 1 | Turning down a chance at power |

## Derangements

A botched Virtue or Willpower roll (Rötschreck, for example) or a
crushing event (killing a loved one in frenzy, being buried alive) can
leave a derangement at the Storyteller's call. A botched degeneration
roll always gives one. Play them as a pattern of triggers and
reactions agreed with the Storyteller, not as comic randomness.

| Derangement | At the table |
|---|---|
| Bipolar Disorder | After a failure, the ST may secretly roll Willpower (diff 8); on a failure, depression. A botch or blood pool below 2 causes it automatically. Depressed: Willpower halved, no blood spent on Attributes. Afterwards, a manic phase: +1 difficulty to resist frenzy |
| Bulimia | Gets hungry faster. When feeding, roll Conscience/Conviction (diff 7) or keep feeding until the blood pool is full. Kept from feeding: frenzy roll (diff 6) |
| Fugue | Under heavy stress, roll Willpower (diff 8). On a failure the character goes into a trance and acts to remove the stress, with no memory of it afterwards |
| Hysteria | Frenzy roll whenever stressed (diff 6, or 8 for sudden or severe stress). Any botch causes automatic frenzy |
| Megalomania | Driven to rise above everyone, coterie included. A real chance to diablerize a stronger Kindred needs Willpower (diff 9) to resist |
| Multiple Personalities | Player and ST agree on the extra personas and their triggers. Each may have its own Abilities, even Virtues |
| Obsessive-Compulsive | A fixed set of rituals followed above all else. 1 Willpower suspends it for a scene |
| Paranoia | +1 difficulty on all social interaction; trusts no one |
| Sanguinary Animism | Believes feeding takes the victim's soul. After feeding, hears the victim's voice; a failed roll lets it act like a second, accusing personality |
| Schizophrenia | When their inner conflict is triggered, +3 difficulty to resist or direct frenzy and -3 dice on Willpower rolls |

*Source: V20 pp. 289-292*

## Running Morality at the Table

- **Make the Hierarchy visible.** Players should know which acts
  their character's rating cares about. The horror is in choosing the
  sin, not in being surprised by a roll.
- **Degeneration is the slow horror of the game.** Pace it: one or
  two meaningful checks a session hit harder than a roll after every
  fight.
- **Low Humanity changes the scenes.** Describe how mortals react to
  a vampire at Humanity 3; use the bearing modifier.
- **Paths are a story, not a mechanic.** Earning one takes a year of
  study and an atrocity. In a Camarilla or Anarch chronicle, a Path
  follower is usually an NPC, and a disturbing one.
- **Derangements need a trigger.** Agree with the player what sets
  the derangement off, then play it straight.

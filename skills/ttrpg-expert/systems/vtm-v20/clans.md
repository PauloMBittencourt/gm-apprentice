# Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary -- Clans

The 13 clans of the V20 core book, the clanless Caitiff, and short
tables of the bloodlines and Sabbat lines from Chapter Ten. Each clan
lists its nickname, usual sect, the three **clan Disciplines** (the
only ones starting Discipline dots can buy, and cheaper with
experience), and its **weakness**.

Powers: `systems/vtm-v20/disciplines.md`. Sects:
`systems/vtm-v20/sects.md`.

Page references are to the V20 core book.

## Camarilla Clans

### Brujah

- **Nickname:** Rabble. **Sect:** Camarilla, with a strong pull
  toward the Anarchs; some young Brujah look to the Sabbat.
- **Disciplines:** Celerity, Potence, Presence.
- **Weakness:** passion turns to rage. Rolls to resist or guide
  frenzy are at **+2 difficulty**. A Brujah **can't spend Willpower
  to avoid a frenzy**, though one point can end a frenzy already
  under way.
- **Play:** rebels, idealists, street fighters, failed philosophers.

*Source: V20 p. 51*

### Gangrel

- **Nickname:** Outlanders. **Sect:** nominally Camarilla; a loud
  faction wants no sect at all.
- **Disciplines:** Animalism, Fortitude, Protean.
- **Weakness:** each frenzy leaves a **temporary animal trait**
  (fur, slit eyes, skittishness in crowds), which may replace an
  earlier one. Over time or in extreme cases a trait becomes
  permanent. Each trait should have a mechanical bite (e.g. a Social
  Attribute dot lost while it lasts), agreed with the Storyteller.
- **Play:** loners, wanderers, survivors closer to the Beast.

*Source: V20 p. 55*

### Malkavian

- **Nickname:** Lunatics. **Sect:** Camarilla, tolerated for their
  odd insight.
- **Disciplines:** Auspex, Dementation, Obfuscate.
- **Weakness:** one **permanent derangement** that can never be
  cured. Willpower can hold it back for a scene; other derangements
  come and go as normal.
- **Variant:** in chronicles set before the event called the "Great
  Prank", Malkavians have **Dominate** instead of Dementation (only
  the Sabbat's antitribu had Dementation then).
- **Play:** seers, tricksters, the broken, the frighteningly lucid.

*Source: V20 pp. 61, 436*

### Nosferatu

- **Nickname:** Sewer Rats. **Sect:** nominally Camarilla; many go
  Anarch or autarkis.
- **Disciplines:** Animalism, Obfuscate, Potence.
- **Weakness:** **Appearance 0**, and it can never rise. Rolls that
  use Appearance are hard for them.
- **Play:** information brokers, the hidden, monsters who remember
  being people.

*Source: V20 p. 63*

### Toreador

- **Nickname:** Degenerates. **Sect:** among the Camarilla's firmest
  supporters.
- **Disciplines:** Auspex, Celerity, Presence.
- **Weakness:** faced with something truly remarkable (a person, a
  work of art, a sunrise), a **Self-Control/Instincts roll
  (difficulty 6)**. Failure: enthralled for the scene, able only to
  engage with it. It ends if the object goes away; being wounded
  allows another roll. Entranced Toreador may not even defend
  themselves.
- **Play:** artists, patrons, socialites, the jaded.

*Source: V20 p. 67*

### Tremere

- **Nickname:** Warlocks. **Sect:** one of the pillars of the
  Camarilla.
- **Disciplines:** Auspex, Dominate, Thaumaturgy.
- **Weakness:** the **first drink** of another vampire's blood counts
  as two, so **two drinks** make a full blood bond. Every neonate is
  made to drink the blood of the clan's seven elders soon after the
  Embrace.
- **Play:** blood sorcerers in a strict pyramid of rank and secrets.

*Source: V20 p. 69*

### Ventrue

- **Nickname:** Blue Bloods. **Sect:** claims to have founded the
  Camarilla; often holds princedoms.
- **Disciplines:** Dominate, Fortitude, Presence.
- **Weakness:** can feed on only **one kind of mortal**, chosen with
  the Storyteller at creation and permanent (narrow or broad: younger
  sisters, the devout, people in love...). Other blood, animals
  included, gives no blood points; it comes back up. Vampire blood is
  exempt.
- **Play:** aristocrats, executives, lords of the Masquerade.

*Source: V20 p. 73*

## Sabbat Clans

### Lasombra

- **Nickname:** Keepers. **Sect:** the Sabbat's leading clan; many
  young Lasombra become pack leaders (ducti) or priests.
- **Disciplines:** Dominate, Obtenebration, Potence.
- **Weakness:** **no reflection** in mirrors, water, glass or any
  polished surface.
- **Play:** prelates, schemers, masters of shadow and the Beast.

*Source: V20 p. 59*

### Tzimisce

- **Nickname:** Fiends. **Sect:** old, detached, numerous members of
  the Sabbat.
- **Disciplines:** Animalism, Auspex, Vicissitude.
- **Weakness:** must rest near **two handfuls of native soil** (from
  a place that mattered in mortal life: birthplace, the grave of the
  Embrace). Each night without it **halves every pool**, cumulatively,
  down to one die, until a full day's rest with the soil.
- **Play:** fleshcrafters, scholars, inhuman lords.

*Source: V20 p. 71*

## Independent Clans

### Assamite

- **Nickname:** Assassins. **Sect:** independent; they hire out to the
  sects. Some want an alliance with one of them.
- **Disciplines:** Celerity, Obfuscate, Quietus.
- **Weakness (the Tremere blood curse):** drinking another Kindred's
  blood costs **one unsoakable lethal level per blood point**. An
  attempt at diablerie deals aggravated damage equal to the victim's
  permanent Willpower, and gives no benefit (not even Generation) if
  the Assamite survives. They also **tithe** about 10% of their
  contract earnings to their elders.
- **Play:** killers, judges, scholars of an old blood cult.

*Source: V20 p. 49*

### Followers of Set

- **Nickname:** Setites, Serpents. **Sect:** no sect wants them, and
  they want none.
- **Disciplines:** Obfuscate, Presence, Serpentis.
- **Weakness:** **+2 health levels** of damage from sunlight, and
  **−1 die** to actions in bright light (spotlights, stage lights,
  flares).
- **Play:** corrupters, cultists, dealers in every vice.

*Source: V20 p. 53*

### Giovanni

- **Nickname:** Necromancers. **Sect:** independent; a family business
  as much as a clan.
- **Disciplines:** Dominate, Necromancy, Potence.
- **Weakness:** their Kiss is agony. Feeding on a mortal deals
  **twice the usual damage** (one blood point taken = two health
  levels), so vessels can die of shock. Many prefer blood banks.
- **Play:** a mafia of the dead; family first, always.

*Source: V20 p. 57*

### Ravnos

- **Nickname:** Deceivers. **Sect:** independent, for lack of a better
  option.
- **Disciplines:** Animalism, Chimerstry, Fortitude.
- **Weakness:** each Ravnos has a **vice** (lying, theft, cruelty...).
  Given the chance to indulge it, they must, unless the player makes a
  **Self-Control/Instincts roll (difficulty 6)**.
- **Play:** tricksters, wanderers, illusionists, con artists.

*Source: V20 p. 65*

## Caitiff

### Caitiff

- **Clanless** vampires, usually of high Generation, whose blood is
  too thin to pass on a clan's traits, or whose sires abandoned them.
- **Disciplines:** no clan Disciplines; starting dots go to any
  Disciplines the Storyteller allows. **All Disciplines cost current
  rating × 6** experience.
- **Weakness:** none from blood. Socially, every clan looks down on
  them.
- The Sabbat's clanless are called **Panders** and follow the same
  rules.

*Source: V20 pp. 33, 124, 430*

## Bloodlines

Smaller lineages from Chapter Ten. Most are rare or need the
Storyteller's leave; several are extinct and only fit historical
games.

| Bloodline | Nickname | Disciplines | Weakness in brief |
|-----------|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Baali | Demons | Daimoinon, Obfuscate, Presence | Can't face or touch holy objects (they burn); True Faith effects doubled |
| Blood Brothers | Frankensteins | Fortitude, Potence, Sanguinus | Can't Embrace; share each other's wound penalties |
| Daughters of Cacophony | Sirens | Fortitude, Melpominee, Presence | Always hear music |
| Gargoyles | Slaves | Flight, Fortitude, Potence, Visceratika | Appearance 0; Willpower counts 2 lower against mind control |
| Harbingers of Skulls | Lazarenes | Auspex, Fortitude, Necromancy | Corpse-like; Appearance 0, Appearance rolls fail |
| Kiasyd | Weirdlings | Dominate, Mytherceria, Obtenebration | Touching iron risks frenzy; cold iron does aggravated damage |
| Nagaraja | Flesh-Eaters | Auspex, Dominate, Necromancy | Need raw flesh as well as blood |
| Salubri | Cyclops | Auspex, Fortitude, Obeah | Lose a Willpower point when feeding on an unwilling vessel |
| Samedi | Stiffs | Fortitude, Obfuscate, Thanatosis | Putrid; Appearance 0, Appearance rolls fail |
| True Brujah | Sages | Potence, Presence, Temporis | Conscience/Conviction rolls +2 difficulty; morality costs double experience |

**Extinct or vanished lines** (historical chronicles): Ahrimanes
(Animalism, Presence, Spiritus), Anda (Animalism, Fortitude,
Protean), Cappadocians (Auspex, Fortitude, Necromancy), Children of
Osiris (as their original clan, plus Bardo), Lamia (Fortitude,
Necromancy, Potence), Lhiannan (Animalism, Ogham, Presence), Noiad
(Animalism, Auspex, Protean).

*Source: V20 pp. 396-426*

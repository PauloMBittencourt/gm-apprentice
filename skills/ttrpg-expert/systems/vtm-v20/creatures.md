# Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary -- Creatures and Others

Everything in the night that isn't a vampire: ghouls, mortal hunters
and True Faith, werewolves, mages, faeries, ghosts, demons, and
animals. Use it to stat an opposing NPC quickly and to remember what
each kind of creature can and can't do to Kindred.

The core book keeps these deliberately sketchy: other beings use
**"equivalent Disciplines"** to stand in for their powers, and a
Humanity score only as a rough measure. Keep them mysterious --
"Vampire" is about vampires.

Combat and damage rules are in `rules-reference.md`; Disciplines in
`disciplines.md`.

## Ghouls

Mortals (or animals) fed vampire blood. They gain power and stop
ageing, but become addicts and, after enough drinks, blood-bound
servants. A master is called a **domitor**.

### Types

| Type | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **Vassal** | The usual ghoul: bound to a domitor, often barely aware of what's going on |
| **Independent** | Masterless -- often orphaned when a domitor died; gets vitae by preying on vampires or selling daytime services. Kindred distrust them |
| **Revenant** | Hereditary ghoul families that make their own vitae; insular, decadent, mostly Sabbat. The book names families such as Bratovitch, Ducheski, Grimaldi and Zantosa |

### Building a Ghoul (Summary)

| Step | Ghoul value |
|---|---|
| Attributes | 6/4/3 (plus 1 free dot each) |
| Abilities | 11/7/4, none above 3 before freebies |
| Disciplines | **Potence 1** free, plus 1 dot of another Discipline (no clan-exclusive ones like Thaumaturgy or Protean; a vassal picks one their domitor knows) |
| Backgrounds | 5 |
| Virtues | 7 (5 for revenants and Sabbat ghouls); never a Path |
| Humanity / Willpower | Conscience + Self-Control / Courage |
| Blood pool | Starts with 1 point; holds 2+ depending on age |
| Freebies | 21 (Merits/Flaws up to 7) |

**Discipline cap by donor's Generation:** 13th-8th → level 1; 7th → 2;
6th → 3; 5th → 4. A ghoul learns only Disciplines its latest donor
has, but once learned any vitae powers them. Six months without vitae
costs a dot, then another each month.

Experience costs for ghouls are in the book (p. 498). An Embraced
ghoul keeps its Disciplines (including Potence) and is topped up to
vampire starting values.

*Source: V20 pp. 496-499*

### Ghoul Rules

- **Blood bond:** slower than for vampires. First drink: strong
  feelings, roleplay only. Second drink: counts as Vinculum 5. A ghoul
  with Willpower 5 or less may need Self-Control rolls to refuse a
  third. A ghoul can wean itself off: after a dry spell of 12 months
  minus 1 per Willpower dot, the bond drops a step, and so on.
- **Ageing:** missing the monthly dose restarts ageing. Past a natural
  lifespan (100-250 years) it ages ten times faster; past 250 years it
  crumbles to dust.
- **Elder ghouls:** a revenant gains +1 blood pool per century; other
  ghouls +1 per two centuries. Regeneration gets easier with age.
- **Healing:** spends blood like a vampire. Regrowing a part: 1
  Willpower, 1-3 blood (finger to whole limb), Stamina diff 8; a
  failure means it never grows back.
- **Frenzy:** the Storyteller decides what provokes it; triggers are
  more common but difficulties lower than for vampires. To resist,
  roll Self-Control; each success delays it a turn, 5 total ends it.
- **Clan taint:** after 5+ blood points from certain clans, Stamina
  diff 5 or pick up a mild version of their weakness until the source
  changes: Brujah (vampire-level frenzy difficulties), Setite (-1
  pool rash in sunlight), Malkavian (mild derangement), Nosferatu
  (-1 or -2 Appearance), Ventrue (craves Ventrue blood).
- **Overdose:** can hold extra blood up to its Stamina. Beyond that,
  Stamina diff 8 or take 1 lethal per extra point and spend a scene
  vomiting it up. While overdosed: +1 Dexterity, vampire-level frenzy,
  and Perception + Self-Control each scene to avoid hallucinations.
- **Withdrawal:** lasts (6 - Stamina) weeks. Self-Control diff 7 to
  resist any chance at vitae; each week Intelligence + Self-Control
  diff 6 or the craving turns to human blood, flesh or sex (1
  Willpower resists; a botch means acting on it).
- **Animal ghouls:** a monthly dose of 1+ blood point gives an animal
  Willpower, Potence 1, a usable blood pool of 2 and trained
  Abilities.
- **War ghouls:** Tzimisce-made monsters. **Szlachta** are fleshcrafted,
  often lobotomised ghouls, too monstrous ever to return to society
  and fiercely loyal. A **vozhd** is a giant made from 15+ ghouls
  grafted together; the Sabbat aims it at a target and runs. A
  lobotomised vozhd is immune to Animalism, Dominate and Presence.
  Use them as rare horrors.

*Source: V20 pp. 387, 499-503*

## Mortal Hunters

Most mortals never learn the truth. Those who do -- and live -- can be
deadly, because they act by day and work in groups.

| Group | What they are | Sample threat |
|---|---|---|
| **Society of Leopold** | The Inquisition, alive and secret; Catholic witch-hunters, now led by a harsh Inquisitor General who brought torture back | Inquisitor: Willpower 9, Humanity anywhere from 5 to 10, Leadership/Occult/Melee 3; sword-cane, stakes, torch; some have True Faith |
| **U.S. Government** | Scattered agencies; the NSA can detect vampires and ghouls with special scanners; the FBI has a small special-affairs department | Agent: Willpower 7, Investigation 4, Firearms 3; heavy pistol, surveillance gear |
| **The Arcanum** | Occult scholars; study rather than fight, but sometimes tip off the Inquisition | Scholar: Willpower 7, Academics/Occult 4; library, money |
| **Criminals** | Mobs and gangs used by Kindred as tools -- lately being told the truth about their masters | Enforcer: Willpower 6, Firearms/Streetwise 3+; SMG, body armour |

*Source: V20 pp. 368-377*

### True Faith

A rare Trait (1-5) of real conviction, not tied to any one religion,
and not usually bought with experience.

| Faith | Effect against vampires |
|---|---|
| 1 | Brandishing a symbol or praying: roll Faith vs the vampire's current Willpower; each success forces it back a step. A holy symbol pressed to its skin does 1 aggravated level per success |
| 2 | Can spend Willpower to resist Dominate and similar mind control for a few turns |
| 3 | Can sense nearby vampires in quiet, peaceful moments; can learn Awareness |
| 4 | Can't be made a ghoul; immune to mind-affecting Disciplines (Dominate, Presence, Obfuscate) |
| 5 | Their prayer or touch can make a vampire flee or collapse in terror and pain |

Aura Perception shows Faith as a silver or gold halo. Holy objects
are useless in the hands of someone without Faith. A vampire can
have Faith only through the True Faith Merit.

*Source: V20 pp. 372-373*

## Werewolves (Lupines)

Shapeshifters who hold the wilderness between cities and have been at
war with the Kindred forever. One werewolf can beat a coterie of
young vampires.

- **Forms:** human, wolf, and mixed forms. The nine-foot **battle
  form** doubles Physical Attributes and causes terror, but Social
  Attributes only work on wolves and other werewolves (except to
  frighten).
- **Delirium:** mortals who see a battle form flee and later explain
  it away -- a natural Masquerade.
- **Speed:** often 2-6 actions a turn, like Celerity. Frenzy as easily
  as Brujah, ignoring wound penalties.
- **Regeneration:** a health level per turn. Only fire, silver and
  supernatural teeth and claws (vampire fangs included) cause lasting
  harm, and they soak all damage, aggravated included, with Stamina
  (but, being alive, they don't halve bashing).
- **Silver** can't be soaked and heals slowly. Wolfsbane does nothing.
- **Gnosis** fuels their powers instead of blood; they can step into
  the spirit world and appear from nowhere.
- **Their blood:** a werewolf holds 20+ blood points' worth. Each
  point drunk adds +1 to the vampire's difficulty to resist frenzy
  and Rötschreck while it lasts.

| Template | Equivalent Disciplines | Willpower | Gnosis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adolescent | Celerity 3, Potence 1, Protean 4 | 5 | 4 |
| Veteran | Celerity 4, Potence 2, Protean 4 | 7 | 6 |
| Elder | Celerity 6, Potence 3, Protean 4, Fortitude 2, Obfuscate 3, Dominate 2, +1 other at 4 | 9 | 8 |

The book also hints at other shapeshifters (ravens, sharks, great
cats, spiders). Keep them as rumours.

*Source: V20 pp. 377-379*

## Mages

Mortals who rewrite reality. Even elders don't understand them.

- **Model** their magic as a set of equivalent Disciplines --
  Thaumaturgy, Auspex, Dominate, Presence, Protean, Obfuscate and
  Obtenebration are common.
- **Mortal body:** can soak bashing only, unless protected by magic
  or cybernetics; heal with time or healing magic.
- **Usually one action a turn.** Willpower 6-10. Spend **Quintessence**
  instead of blood.
- **Can be blood-bound;** the Embrace destroys their magic forever.
- **Paradox:** a natural "1" on a blatant spell (flight, fireballs)
  backfires horribly.
- **Countermagic:** a vampire with Thaumaturgy (or Thaumaturgical
  Countermagic) can roll Wits + Occult, diff 6, against a spell aimed
  at them; each success cancels one of the mage's.

| Template | Equivalent Disciplines | Willpower | Quintessence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Young cultist | Auspex 2, Dominate 2, Presence 1, Protean 1, Thaumaturgy 3 | 5 | 10 |
| Technological abomination | Auspex 2, Dominate 2, Fortitude 4, Potence 3, Presence 3 | 8 | 10 |
| High wizard | Auspex 4, Chimerstry 3, Dominate 2, Fortitude 2, Obfuscate 4, Presence 3, Thaumaturgy 5 | 9 | 12 |

*Source: V20 pp. 379-382*

## Faeries

Wild, capricious beings hidden in human form. Vampires who deal with
them rarely come out ahead. Activity peaks at solstices, equinoxes
and festivals; some Toreador clash with them over artists.

- Heal like mortals; soak lethal but don't halve bashing; only **fire**
  does aggravated damage. **Cold (unforged) iron** can't be soaked.
- Dislike vampires and avoid them; can hide their true form, though
  Auspex often sees through it.
- Spend **Glamour** on equivalent Disciplines. Their blood may taste
  ordinary, or bring bliss and visions.

| Template | Equivalent Disciplines | Willpower | Glamour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pooka trickster | Animalism 2, Auspex 2, Celerity 2, Chimerstry 3, Obfuscate 4, Protean 4 | 6 | 6 |
| Redcap brute | Celerity 3, Fortitude 2, Obfuscate 3, Potence 2 | 5 | 5 |
| Sidhe enchantress | Celerity 1, Chimerstry 5, Dominate 4, Obfuscate 4, Presence 5 (Appearance 7) | 7 | 10 |

*Source: V20 pp. 382-384*

## Ghosts (Wraiths)

- **Feed on emotion** to refill a **Passion** pool -- a frenzying
  vampire can make a ghost stronger.
- Strongest in **haunted places** where suffering left a mark, where
  the Shroud is thin.
- **Can't be fought normally.** Reach them through their **fetters**
  (people or objects they cling to) or with Necromancy; they can
  possess mortals.
- **Invisible** unless they choose otherwise; Necromancy (and sometimes
  Auspex) sees them.
- **Spectres** are malicious ghosts that live to cause pain.
- Weak in the living world, dangerous in their own (the Shadowlands).

| Template | Equivalent Disciplines | Willpower | Passion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recently deceased | Auspex 1, Chimerstry 1, Dementation 2, Dominate 1, Vicissitude 1 | 5 | 5 |
| Spectre | Chimerstry 4, Dementation 4, Vicissitude 4, Obtenebration 3, others 2 | 7 | 9 |
| Old soul | Dementation 4, Dominate 3, Thaumaturgy 3, Vicissitude 3, others 1-2 | 9 | 10 |

*Source: V20 pp. 384-385*

## Demons

- **Need a host.** The lesser **Fallen** possess mortals with weak
  ties to their bodies; the mighty **Earthbound** dwell in objects or
  places (reliquaries). Without a host they are dragged back to Hell.
- Can reveal an **apocalyptic form** (wings, horns, claws); Aura
  Perception can show it.
- **Immune to mind control,** but bound by any bargain struck when
  summoned.
- Fallen powers equal Disciplines at 1-5; Earthbound at 6+.
- **Feed on faith:** worship and pacts from mortals fuel them.
  Cultists may wield granted powers.
- **Tormented** by memories and the pull of their prison, which makes
  them erratic.

*Source: V20 pp. 385-387*

Animals have Physical and Mental Attributes only; Abilities in
brackets in the book come from training. Most animals fear Kindred.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rat | 1/2/3 | Bite 1 | 3 | 1/4 |
| Bat | 1/3/2 | Bite 1 | 3 | 1/4 |
| House cat | 1/3/3 | Bite 1, claw 1 | 4 | 1 |
| Small bird | 1/3/2 | Harassment (-1 die to the target) | 4 | 1/4 |
| Large bird (hawk, raven) | 2/3/3 | Claw 2 | 5 | 1/2 |
| Medium dog | 2/3/3 | Bite 3, claw 2 | 5 | 2 |
| Large dog | 4/3/3 | Bite 5, claw 4 | 6 | 2 |

"Blood 1/4" means four animals make one blood point. The book also
covers camels, chimpanzees, pigs, constrictors, spiders and more.

### Packs and Swarms

Roll the swarm, not each animal: one damage roll per target per turn
(diff 6), which the character can dodge or soak. Damage dice range
from 1 (small bugs) through 3 (rats) and 4 (birds, bats, feral cats)
to 6 (wild dogs, wolves).

- **Movement:** dodging lets the character move normally; otherwise
  the swarm halves their movement.
- **Overrun:** more than 3 levels of damage in a turn (after soak), or
  a botch, knocks the character down -- they can crawl only 1-2 yards
  a turn and the swarm's difficulty drops to 5.
- **Fighting back:** the swarm disperses after losing its health
  levels and dies after two more. Guns and small weapons kill one
  animal per hit; shotguns, SMGs, big weapons and fire do full damage.
  Swarms don't soak.

Health levels and initiative for each swarm type are in the book.

*Source: V20 pp. 387-392*

## Using Others at the Table

- **Mortals are the real danger to the Masquerade.** A hunter cell
  with a day-time plan is scarier than a werewolf.
- **One werewolf is a whole session.** Use them to make the city's
  edges dangerous, not as random encounters.
- **Keep mages, fae, ghosts and demons strange.** Give them a want, a
  rule they follow and a price for dealing with them, and never
  explain all of it.
- **Ghouls are the coterie's daytime face.** A domitor's ghoul is
  leverage -- for the PCs and against them.

# Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary -- Weapons and Armour

Weapon and armour ratings for V20. Use them when equipping characters
and for damage lookups in combat. How attacks, soak, autofire and
targeting work is in `rules-reference.md`.

## The One Rule to Remember

**Guns and blunt weapons do bashing damage to vampires** unless the
attacker targets the head; then it's lethal. Against mortals, guns are
lethal. Blades (knives, swords, axes, stakes) are lethal to both.
Only fire, sunlight and supernatural fangs and claws do aggravated
damage. That is why Kindred prefer blades and why a gunfight rarely
kills one.

*Source: V20 pp. 280-281*

## Melee Weapons

| Weapon | Damage | Type | Conceal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sap | Strength +1 | Bashing (lethal to the head) | P |
| Club | Strength +2 | Bashing (lethal to the head) | T |
| Knife | Strength +1 | Lethal | J |
| Sword | Strength +2 | Lethal | T |
| Axe | Strength +3 | Lethal | N |
| Stake | Strength +1 | Lethal | T |

**Stake through the heart:** target the heart (difficulty 9) and score
at least 3 damage successes to paralyse a vampire (full rules in
`rules-reference.md`, "Stake Through the Heart").

**Improvising:** for objects not on the list, pick the closest match
(a bottle is a sap, a pipe a club, a machete a sword) and use
Strength + Melee to hit.

*Source: V20 p. 280*

## Ranged Weapons

| Type | Damage | Range | Rate | Capacity | Conceal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revolver, light (.38) | 4 | 12 | 3 | 6 | P |
| Revolver, heavy (.44 Magnum) | 6 | 35 | 2 | 6 | J |
| Pistol, light (9mm) | 4 | 20 | 4 | 15+1 | P |
| Pistol, heavy (.45) | 5 | 25 | 3 | 13+1 | J |
| Rifle (hunting) | 8 | 200 | 1 | 3+1 | N |
| SMG, small* | 4 | 20 | 3 | 17+1 | J |
| SMG, large* | 4 | 50 | 3 | 30+1 | T |
| Assault rifle* | 7 | 150 | 3 | 30+1 | N |
| Shotgun (pump) | 8 | 20 | 1 | 5+1 | T |
| Shotgun, semi-auto | 8 | 20 | 3 | 6+1 | T |
| Crossbow** | 5 | 20 | 1 | 1 | T |

\* Can fire three-round bursts, full auto and sprays (see
`rules-reference.md`, "Ranged Combat Maneuvers").
\** Takes five turns to reload. Bashing to Kindred unless aimed at the
head or heart -- the usual way to try a stake from range.

**Reading the columns:**

- **Damage:** the damage dice pool (no Strength added).
- **Range:** normal range in yards or metres. Up to double is long
  range, at difficulty 8.
- **Rate:** the most shots (or three-round bursts) per turn; doesn't
  apply to full auto or sprays.
- **Capacity:** rounds held; "+1" is one in the chamber.
- **Conceal:** P = pocket, J = jacket, T = trenchcoat, N = can't be
  hidden.

*Source: V20 p. 281*

## Armour

| Class | Example | Rating | Penalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| One | Reinforced clothing | 1 | 0 |
| Two | Armoured T-shirt | 2 | 1 |
| Three | Kevlar vest | 3 | 1 |
| Four | Flak jacket | 4 | 2 |
| Five | Full riot gear | 5 | 3 |

- **Rating** adds to soak against bashing, lethal, and aggravated
  damage from fangs and claws. **Never against fire or sunlight.**
- **Penalty** comes off dice pools that need agility (most
  Dexterity-based pools).
- Attackers can target a gap to ignore the armour, usually at +1 or
  +2 difficulty.
- Damage dice of at least twice the rating in one attack destroy the
  armour (`rules-reference.md`, "Armour").

*Source: V20 p. 280*

## Equipment at the Table

- **Readying a weapon** (drawing it, or slapping in a prepared clip)
  takes the turn. Doing it alongside another action means splitting
  the dice pool and rolling Dexterity + Melee or Firearms (diff 4).
- **Concealment matters** in Elysium and on the street: a shotgun
  under a trenchcoat is a Masquerade risk the moment it comes out.
- **Ammunition is rarely tracked** unless the scene is about running
  out. Track it in a siege or a long chase.
- **Mortals with guns are still dangerous:** bashing damage stacks,
  and an Incapacitated vampire collapses and can only spend blood to
  heal -- easy prey for whoever brought the stake.
- **Special ammunition, explosives and vehicles** aren't detailed in
  the core book's charts. Improvise from the nearest entry and note
  the ruling.

*Source: V20 pp. 257, 280-281*
